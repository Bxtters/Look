    Why these levers

    Bone structure is mostly fixed. Ratings still move with sleep, skin, grooming, expression, and body composition.

    Sleep & recovery

    Well-rested faces are rated more attractive and healthier; fatigue shows in under-eyes and droop within hours.

    Axelsson et al. / sleep & facial attractiveness

    Skin clarity

    Even tone and low inflammation are strong cross-cultural cues — SPF and gentle care beat unproven face exercises.

    Dermatology; Fink / Jones skin homogeneity

    Expression & grooming

    Genuine smiles and sharp grooming change ratings without changing bone — often the largest controllable deltas.
