    On-device account

    LOOK uses a local vault: display name + passphrase stay on this browser. Scores are encrypted with a key derived from your passphrase (PBKDF2 + AES-GCM). No email, no cloud login, no face images stored or uploaded — designed to avoid shipping biometric-adjacent data to a server.
